Email notifications

?

Gmail needs a 2-Step Verification app password. Generate one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords — first check the account avatar in the top-right corner is the account you put in "Sender account" below; signing in as the wrong Google account is the most common reason a test fails. ?

Install the free ntfy app (iOS or Android, no account needed) and subscribe to a topic with a long, random name, then paste https://ntfy.sh/<topic> here. The topic name is effectively a password — anyone who knows it can read your notifications, so keep it unguessable. Self-hosted ntfy servers work too.

Telegram

?

Message @BotFather on Telegram, send /newbot, and follow its prompts — it hands you a bot token when it's done. Paste that token below and save.

To pair your Telegram account, open a private chat with your new bot and send /start <your web token> — the same token you sign in to this dashboard with (see Access below, or your login screen). After pairing, delete your /start message — it contains your web token. The bot tries to delete it for you automatically, but it may lack permission to.

Once paired, the bot mirrors your web chat both ways: messages you send here also go to Telegram, and vice versa.

Token changes take effect after a restart, not immediately on save — the poller keeps using the old token until then.
