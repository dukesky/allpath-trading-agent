Email notifications

?

Gmail needs a 2-Step Verification app password. Generate one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords — first check the account avatar in the top-right corner is the account you put in "Sender account" below; signing in as the wrong Google account is the most common reason a test fails. Copy the password straight from the dialog — pasting it with Gmail's grouping spaces is fine, the form strips them. The sender account and the account that generated the password must match, and changing the Google account password revokes all app passwords issued under it.

Using a different provider? Set the SMTP host, port, and enable STARTTLS as your provider documents.

Notifications report what happened; they never contain action links.

Push notifications

?

Install the free ntfy app (iOS or Android, no account needed) and subscribe to a topic with a long, random name, then paste https://ntfy.sh/<topic> here. The topic name is effectively a password — anyone who knows it can read your notifications, so keep it unguessable. Self-hosted ntfy servers work too.
